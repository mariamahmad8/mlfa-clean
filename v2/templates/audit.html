Audit logi
Audit log — what each action means
Auto
The AI handled the email on its own — sorted it, filed it, and sent any forward or reply — with nobody reviewing it.
Queued
The email went to the hub for someone to look at, because automation is off for this inbox or because no category fitted it.
Queued (AI off)
It went to the hub without a category, because AI sorting is off for this inbox. Someone picks the category by hand.
Approved
Someone approved it in the hub, and the system then did the filing, forwarding, and reply for its category. Your current category settings are used — not the ones from when it arrived.
Rejected
Someone rejected it and gave a reason. It went to Trash, and no reply or forward was sent.
Dismissed
Someone decided it needed nothing — junk, or not for us. Marked as read, with no reply and no forward.
Expired
Nobody reviewed it in time, so the hub let it go. It got a queue_expired label in Outlook, and was never filed, forwarded, or replied to.
Created / Updated / Deleted
Someone on your team added, edited, or removed a category, reply, recipient, user, or inbox.
